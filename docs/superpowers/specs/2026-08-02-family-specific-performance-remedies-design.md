## 2. Value parser: `remedies-cap-parse.js`

New pure module, the `measurement-date-parse.js` /
`termination-fee-parse.js` contract shape: typed `{outcome:'RESOLVED',
canonical_value, matched_text, currency}` or `{outcome:'ABSTAIN', reason}`
— never a throw on prose, never arithmetic, never repair.
`REMEDIES_CAP_PARSE_VERSION` threaded into the resolution receipt (P1 M-6).
One exported function:

**`parseLiabilityCapAmount(quote)`** — the TERMF/consideration inversion
adopted verbatim: counts ONLY currency-prefixed literals; bare numerals
never contaminate the count. This matters concretely in this family's own
fixtures: "the twentieth (20th) business day", "within five Business Days",
section refs "Sections 8.01 and 8.02", and the statute citation
"6 Del. C. § 2708" (a quasi-numeric token that breaks a naive extract-all-
numbers regex — evidence-pack §4) all survive in real quotes and are all
invisible to this tokenizer.

- Candidate token: `[$€£]` optionally followed by whitespace, then a
  maximal digit-comma-dot run. Section-reference/date/clock-time exclusions
  inherited from the P1 grammar (LTR-mark-tolerant; matched against LITERAL
  committed fixture bytes, never retyped ASCII).
- Exactly one surviving `$` literal with STRICT 3-digit grouping
  (`^\d{1,3}(,\d{3})*(\.\d+)?$` after `$`-strip) → RESOLVED; canonical
  form: strip `$`, strip grouping commas, preserve any decimal as-written
  (`'60000000'`); must round-trip `canonicalValueAllowed`'s
  `NON_NEGATIVE_DECIMAL_STRING` regex; `currency: 'USD'`. **Added
  2026-08-03 per audit finding m2:** the grouping regex above admits
  leading-zero literals (e.g. "$060,000" strips to `'060000'`), which
  `NON_NEGATIVE_DECIMAL_STRING`'s `/^(0|[1-9]\d*)(\.\d+)?$/` rejects — a
  round-trip failure with no prior typed reason code. Any canonical value
  that fails the `NON_NEGATIVE_DECIMAL_STRING` round-trip → ABSTAIN
  `CANONICAL_ROUND_TRIP_FAILED` (no repair, same as `MALFORMED_GROUPING`).
- Malformed grouping → ABSTAIN `MALFORMED_GROUPING` (no repair).
- `€`/`£` prefix → ABSTAIN `NON_USD_CURRENCY` (no FX, ever) → review.
- Two or more surviving money literals → ABSTAIN `MULTIPLE_MONEY_LITERALS`
  (compound quotes are N claims; the producer splits; the parser never
  picks — the IOC limb rule verbatim).
- Zero money literals → ABSTAIN `NO_MONEY_LITERAL` — the fee-keyed-cap
  case (`22180602…`), the family's designed dominant review class.
- Spelled-out money with no `$` literal → ABSTAIN `NON_LITERAL_MONEY`.
  The belt-and-braces form "two hundred four million dollars
  ($204,000,000)" parses on the `$` literal — but that grounded instance
  (deal `6369cc9c…`) is FEE prose, and the cap corroboration gate (section
  4), not the parser, is what stops it minting a cap claim; test 2 pins
  the pairing.
- NO zero-pattern table: a genuine $0 liability cap is not
  corpus-attested; "shall have any liability or obligation … other than
  for fraud" is NON_RECOURSE territory, never a zero cap.

No other parser ships. The SP, bond-waiver, non-recourse and jury claims
are presence claims gated by allowed-values membership + corroboration —
the closing-conditions "no parser is a ruled decision" precedent applies,
and every resolved presence/enum claim carries the honest
`buildMechanicalAnswerProvenance({ extraPins: { gate:
'ALLOWED_VALUES_MEMBERSHIP' } })` tag construction (uniform, one
tag-construction story per family; NO auto-pass-block entry rides on it —
that block is the closing-conditions family's Ben-approved bring-down
scope, not a family-generic device).

## Known costs, stated up front

- **Fee-keyed caps are the parent-side corpus norm** (`22180602…`): they
  ABSTAIN `NO_MONEY_LITERAL` and queue, every time. The remedy is Ben
  reading the cap and its fee reference together — never a fee-claim
  lookup, never arithmetic. Expect this to be the family's largest review
  class.
- Compound remedies sections are the norm (four-concept titles,
  grant+bond+election in one paragraph): until producers split limbs
  reliably, `AMBIGUOUS_REMEDY_KIND` volume will be material. Two-strike
  escalation applies to prompt iteration, never to loosening the
  full-table check.
- The enumerated-both-sides mutual SP form (`5c1210a6…`) queues
  `SP_SCOPE_UNCORROBORATED`; group defined terms ("Parent Related
  Parties", "the Company Entities") may queue `PARTY_UNRESOLVED`. Queued
  correct claims beat published inverted ones; live-run handoffs measure
  the rates; new grammatical forms land by reviewed diff.
- `/\bFinancing Sources?\b/` under REM-NONRECOURSE will raise unmatched
  signals inside TERMF-SOLE and financing-covenant sections (receipt-
  verified cross-hits). Accepted: it is the financing-source shield's
  strongest tell; deletion asymmetry applies; the rate is measured from
  queue data.
- Bare "Remedies"/"Enforcement" titles and the 173-card null-subtype
  population make classifier coverage the family's biggest recall risk;
  stage-2 AI classification is flagged provenance and never auto-passes
  unverified — under-coverage is typed and measurable, never silent.
- REM-JURY presence claims inherit rank 25 under the single proposed
  tier — over-ranked boilerplate, named in the Ben flag; a future tier
  split is a one-line reviewed diff.
- Case-sensitive defined-term patterns (`Non-Recourse`, `Liability
  Limitation`, `Financing Sources`) miss lower-case drafting variants;
  priced — the case-insensitive phrase patterns cover the prose shapes,
  and a miss costs a missed veto, never a wrong claim.
