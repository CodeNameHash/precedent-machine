# IOC remaining coverage

## Decision

Capture deferred IOC mechanisms as byte-verified open-world evidence. Do not
add governed claims or duplicate qualifier ownership.

## Corpus basis

The IOC corpus has 1,158 cards across 40 deals. The existing family design
puts the long tail, affirmative covenants, consent and efforts standards,
ordinary-course and related exceptions, and notice windows out of scope.
966 cards, about 83%, are cross references. Quote-local extraction must not
resolve values that live only in a referenced clause.

## Scope

The producer captures affirmative covenants, long-tail restrictions, consent
or efforts standards, exceptions, and thresholds or notice windows. Every
item remains `OPEN_WORLD_PROPOSITION` with `canonical_value: null`.

The side is not model asserted. The existing parent-section inheritance rule
owns target or buyer side. Legacy `party_scope` is mutual on all 1,158 cards
and must not be used. No-shop, antitrust efforts, employee matters, and
other qualifier-owning families remain the governing location for promotion.

## Product parity

Review, comparison, and query surfaces are recorded as `OPEN` and
`FOLLOW_ON_REQUIRED` in the parity register. No market result is complete.
