# Merger structure follow-on

## Decision

Capture deferred merger mechanics as exact open-world evidence. Do not
promote them to claims or derive deal structure.

## Corpus basis

The family design records STRUCT-DIRECTORS 38 cards across 30 deals,
STRUCT-EFFECTS 23 across 22 deals, 251(h) 16 cards across 2 deals, and
Schedule 14D-9 33 cards across 9 deals. The corpus contains the statement
that a merger is governed by Section 251(h) of the DGCL. It also has
Top-Up, short-form, and subsequent-offering forms at low deal counts.

## Boundaries

`transaction_steps` remains a separate source. Agreement text cannot infer,
replace, or overwrite it. Consideration, conditions, termination, D&O, and
no-shop facts retain their existing owners.

## Parity and decisions

The review table, compare source, and query registry are in the parity
register as OPEN/FOLLOW_ON_REQUIRED. Ben must decide the future taxonomy for
directors/effects/actions, effective-time variants, statutory short-form
routes, Top-Up thresholds, offer sequence, SEC filings, stockholder lists,
and board designation. Each needs a distinct rule before promotion.
