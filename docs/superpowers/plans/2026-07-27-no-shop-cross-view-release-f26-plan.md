# F26 no-shop cross-view release assembly

## Outcome

Assemble the exact F23 copy-delivery, F24 timing and F25 categorical
certifications into one content-addressed, inactive no-shop candidate release.
Review, Corpus Context, Compare and Query receive one provision-level contract
whose metric subrows retain their separate legal identities and fail locally.

## Contract

1. Preserve the frozen F22-F25 artefacts byte-for-byte.
2. Add one successor central admission resolver. It selects and validates the
   frozen F22/F23, F24 or F25 proof internally. Callers cannot select a lane.
3. Bind all nine exact admissions to one F26 manifest and release ID.
4. Project one no-shop provision row containing:
   - copy delivery;
   - proposal notice;
   - initial and subsequent matching periods;
   - prohibited actions and action knowledge qualifiers;
   - exception prerequisites, exception effects and inline permissions.
5. Preserve raw and canonical duration values, day basis, legal trigger, party,
   clarity, ambiguity and lawyer warnings.
6. Preserve categorical item identities, source lineage, grouping dimensions
   and direct or inferred effect warnings. Preserve prerequisite targets,
   notice dependencies, relationship materialisation state and inline-permission
   scope. Do not merge legal categories.
7. Produce the four surface bindings from the same provision object.
8. Isolate an invalid or unfamiliar subrow without suppressing valid siblings.
9. Compile a bounded release-aware cohort request with one set-based database
   call, zero immediate retries and no broad claims or cards. Validate the
   complete response before caching it; an identical cache hit performs no
   database call.
10. Keep publication, active release, active pointer, corpus write, production
    query and production activation authority at `NONE`.
11. Treat QXO as the selected certification subject, never as its own market
    comparator. Suppress percentages and distributions until at least one
    independent comparable deal is admitted.

## Verification

- Exact envelope, admission, predecessor and content identities.
- Central resolution of all nine admissions and rejection of swaps or omissions.
- Hours remain normalised to elapsed days; business days remain distinct.
- Warnings and categorical grouping dimensions survive all four surfaces.
- One damaged subrow produces one typed failure and leaves siblings renderable.
- One compact request has a corpus-independent database-call budget.
- One cache miss performs one RPC, a cache hit performs none, and poisoned
  release, admission, tuple, count or state results cannot enter the cache.
- Staging-only design preview verifies responsive layout, navigation,
  collapsible market context, keyboard operation and no header overlap.
- Production feature flags and active pointers remain unchanged.
