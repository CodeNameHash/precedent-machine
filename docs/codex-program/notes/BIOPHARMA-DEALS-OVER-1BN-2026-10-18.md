# Biopharma deals over $1bn, 2025 to 2026: candidates for the next intakes

Evidence for Ben's decision (plan finding (166), verbatim: "btw let's do major biopharma deals as the basi sfor the build out of next searches, starting > $1bn"), compiled 2026-10-18 by implementer C on the manager's work package C3. Not a status document; no intake was submitted.

## How the list was made

- Candidates came from four deal trackers read on 2026-10-18: the Biobucks 2026 tracker (73 deals, updated 18 October 2026), the Xtalks 2025 roundup, the Life Science Daily and PharmaDossier 2026 lists, and IQVIA's 2025 outlook, with press releases and the parties' own announcements for the values marked PR below. Every value is a headline figure as its source states it; "EV" marks an enterprise value, "upfront + CVR" a cash price plus a contingent value right.
- Every merger agreement was found by EDGAR full-text search (form 8-K, the target's name and the agreement's title, a window around the announcement), fetched in full, its HTTP status and size recorded, and its first lines read to confirm the parties and the title. "Tender" is read from the agreement's own text ("the Offer" defined and used); "CVR" from "Contingent Value Right" in it; "scheme" from a UK or Irish Transaction Agreement. The scripts are in the session's scratchpad (`find-exhibits.py`, `verify-exhibits.py`); every URL below answered 200 on 2026-10-18.
- "In corpus" is read from `product_analysis_runs.retrieval_url` on the preview branch (six documents: Metsera, Apogee, Arcellx, plus Olaplex, NCS Multistage and Public Storage, which are not biopharma).
- Scope: public targets, biopharma or pharma (therapeutics, including radiopharmaceuticals), announced 1 January 2025 to 18 October 2026, headline value at or above about $1bn. Tools, diagnostics and devices are listed separately at the end, as are deals the intake cannot take.

## What the intake cannot take

- CVC / GBL consortium / Recordati (2026-05-22, about $12.4bn equity, Italian tender offer): no EDGAR filing; Recordati is not an SEC registrant.
- Samsung Biologics / PolyPeptide Group (2026-07-20, about $1.8bn equity, Swiss tender offer): no EDGAR filing.
- The four schemes (Verona, Centessa, Avadel) and the Dutch offer (Merus) have an Exhibit 2.1 on EDGAR, so the intake takes the URL, but the agreement is a Transaction Agreement under English, Irish or Dutch law: the scheme replaces the merger mechanics, closing conditions and termination rights the schema was written for (Court sanction, the Scheme Document, a Panel), and the Dutch offer carries a post-offer reorganisation instead of a second-step merger. They are worth one run each for what the extractor does with them, after the Delaware deals.
- Metsera's Amendment No. 1 (2025-11-07) is an amendment, not a restated agreement; the intake's revision classifier (`lib/agreement-revision-classifier.js`) decides what it does with it. The corpus's generation 8 is the original 2025-09-22 agreement.

## The three to run first, and why

1. **GSK / Nuvalent** (row 5): a two-step tender offer with a second-step merger and no CVR, $10.6bn, Delaware, drafted by GSK's counsel with GSK plc as a limited-purpose party (Section 9.14). The corpus's three biopharma deals are two one-step mergers (Metsera, Apogee) and one tender offer with a CVR (Arcellx); a plain tender offer at this size is the drafting the schema's Termination and No-Shop families were written against and the corpus has not yet run.
2. **Indivior / Supernus** (row 34): the only stock deal, a merger of equals with an exchange ratio, an S-4 and two stockholder votes, a special dividend and a combined-company governance package. Every corpus deal so far is cash; the consideration election and value-terms tables (work packages 13 and 16) and the Votes section have never seen a fixed exchange ratio.
3. **Johnson & Johnson / Intra-Cellular Therapies** (row 1): the largest 2025 deal, a plain one-step cash merger with a stockholder vote, drafted by a different firm from the Metsera and Apogee agreements; the control for the two above, and the one Ben is most likely to have read.

Fourth, for the CVR drafting the corpus already partly covers: **Curium / Lantheus** (row 12, a one-step merger with a $12 CVR and a private-equity-backed acquirer, so a financing covenant and a reverse termination fee are likely) or **Novo Nordisk / Akero** (row 17, a one-step merger with a CVR by a non-US acquirer).
