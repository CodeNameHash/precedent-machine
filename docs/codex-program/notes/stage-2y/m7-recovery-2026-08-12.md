## The one defect carried forward

`REPRESENTATIONS` is corrected here, on the handoff's explicit instruction not to
treat the recovered logic as accepted.

The recovered matcher `/\b(?:authority; binding|authorization; valid and binding)\b/i`
**binds 0 of 7 agreements**. The headings it was written for do not occur in this
corpus. The nearest real ones are `Authority; No Violations; Consents and
Approvals.` (concho), `Authority; Execution and Delivery; Enforceability.`
(metsera) and `Authority.` (modiv); redhat, skechers, topbuild and skywater have
no authority-shaped section heading at all.

Four things were wrong, not one. The first two were found by review of the
binding coverage, the second two by review of the bindings themselves.

1. **The pattern.** Replaced with an attributing form. The bare phrase
   `representations and warranties` stays excluded, because it also heads
   survival, conditions and non-reliance provisions.

2. **The node kinds scanned.** `selectFamilySections` only considered
   `node_kind === 'SECTION'`. Five of the seven agreements carry
   `REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF THE COMPANY` on an **ARTICLE** with plain
   topic headings beneath it, so that shape failed silently as
   `INPUT_NOT_AVAILABLE`. Articles are now searched — **for this family only**.
   Applied generally the backstop was too broad: it bound SkyWater
   `CONSIDERATION` to an exchange-agent mechanic sitting under an "exchange of
   certificates" article. Every other family keeps section-only matching until
   it earns an explicit rule.

3. **Which unit inside the container.** Matching the family regex inside the
   container selected text that *names* the family rather than text that states
   a rule — redhat took a non-reliance disclaimer, topbuild an
   assumption-qualified takeover-statute rep, and the five article-bound deals
   took the disclosure chapeau. REPRESENTATIONS now selects the first
   substantive organisation and existence representation: duly organised,
   incorporated or formed, validly existing, in good standing. All seven
   agreements carry one.

4. **The chapeau.** The governing chapeau qualifies everything beneath it
   ("Except as disclosed…"), so a child sentence read without it reads too
   widely. It is now carried as inherited context on the binding
   (`context_chapeau_node_occurrence_id`), never as the proposition. A
   REPRESENTATIONS unit that cannot be linked to one is routed to review
   (`CHAPEAU_CONTEXT_UNLINKED`), and a disclosure chapeau selected on its own is
   routed to review as well (`DISCLOSURE_CHAPEAU_ONLY`) rather than standing as
   a complete comparison proposition.

Measured across all 25 families and all seven committed M2 indexes, against the
recovered code as baseline: **7 gained, 0 lost, 0 moved.** Seven
REPRESENTATIONS bindings, each the organisation and existence representation
with its governing chapeau linked. No other family changed.

## Selection diagnostic and the review gate

Every binding records how its authored unit was chosen — `AUTHORED_UNIT_MATCH`,
`HEADING_MATCH`, or `DOCUMENT_ORDER_FALLBACK` — so a filtered pick and a
fallback pick are never indistinguishable downstream.

`applySelectionModeGate` routes three cases out of the normal comparison lane
and into review, mirroring `applyAmbiguityGate` exactly, including re-deriving
the proposition identity:

| reason | missing role | diagnostic |
|---|---|---|
| `DOCUMENT_ORDER_FALLBACK` | `FAMILY_MATCHED_AUTHORED_UNIT` | `M7_DOCUMENT_ORDER_FALLBACK_SELECTION` |
| `CHAPEAU_CONTEXT_UNLINKED` | `GOVERNING_CHAPEAU_CONTEXT` | `M7_CHAPEAU_CONTEXT_UNLINKED` |
| `DISCLOSURE_CHAPEAU_ONLY` | `SUBSTANTIVE_LEGAL_UNIT` | `M7_DISCLOSURE_CHAPEAU_ONLY` |

The gate keys on **family plus node**, not node alone: two families can
legitimately bind the same authored unit, and one of them reaching it by
document order must not drag the other into review.

## Displayed actors

An actor is displayed only where a member claim contributes it, or where an
`ACTOR_OR_PARTIES` entry has `provenance_kind === 'DIRECT'` and its span lies
inside the proposition's own authored source. The unprovenanced fallback that
used to sit at the end of `actorTexts` displayed synthetic `obligated_party` and
`control_party` values carrying no span at all, which is what put five
unsupported actors on PROXY_MEETING rows. Those five are gone, and
`tests/stage-2y-structure-actor-support.test.js` passes with **zero pinned
exceptions**: 1,111 rows, every displayed actor supported.

## Still open, and deliberately not decided here

The label-phrase problem is narrowed but not solved. REPRESENTATIONS now has an
explicit rule; the other 24 families still select by matching the family's label
phrase inside the container, which prefers text that names the family over text
that states a rule. Each family needs its own rule to be trusted the way
REPRESENTATIONS now is. The `DOCUMENT_ORDER_FALLBACK` diagnostic makes the
affected bindings visible, and the gate keeps them out of the normal lane, so
the exposure is contained rather than silent.

## Consequences for the M6 and M7 rebuild

**The M6 output-set digest in the handoff is superseded, not matchable.**
`receipts/stage-2y-structure-m6-agreement-projection-row-correction.json` is
`SEALED` and binds `projection_module.sha256` and `runner.sha256`. Its
`output_set_digest` `2323eca1…` hashes projection bytes that embed
`expanded_text`, so correcting rows necessarily breaks it. The recovered
`writeCanonical` edit makes correction mode overwrite differing bytes rather than
fail `EXISTING_OUTPUT_DRIFT`, which is what allows the rebuild to proceed — and
also means drift no longer announces itself in that mode.

**The M7 shadow runner will throw `family input state drift`.**
`scripts/stage-2y-structure-generalisation-shadow.mjs:193-195` compares fresh
`selectFamilySections` output against the `family_input_states` pinned in the
cohort manifest. Eight bindings moved, so the throw is expected on first run and
the manifest must be regenerated through
`scripts/stage-2y-m7-generalisation-cohort-finalise.mjs`, not suppressed.
