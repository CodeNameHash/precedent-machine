## 1. Blind-sample size and composition — derived, not asked

The blind-sample size and composition are derived mechanically from Decision
17's standard: under 1% false publication overall at 95% confidence,
measured at the rendered row, risk-weighted per the plan. No separate size
ruling is required. The stratification over-weights rows capable of
severity-one errors (see the rider below). This closes PLAN.md §20 open
ruling 2 as to method; the derived numbers are recorded when the sample is
frozen.

### Rider — severity-one repair rule (approved 2026-09-01)

Any severity-one error found — party attribution, monetary amount, or
polarity (does/does not, shall/shall not) — triggers root-cause repair of
the producing rule before publication continues, at any observed rate. A
severity-one error is a defect to fix, never a statistic absorbed into the
1% headroom. The 1% gate itself is unchanged.
