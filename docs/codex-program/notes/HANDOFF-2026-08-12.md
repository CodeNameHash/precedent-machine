## Why lawyer review stopped

Ben found that the comparison text repeated the source clause. A full check
confirmed the issue on all 38 source-to-row review cards. The first correction
changed the row into labelled parts such as Rule, Applies to, Timing and
Qualifications. M6 and M7 were regenerated.

The replacement review then exposed two further defects:

1. Applies to could collect actor phrases from surrounding text, even when the
   phrases did not govern the selected rule.
2. The deterministic family matcher could select the wrong authored unit. One
   specific-performance card selected governing-law text because both appeared
   in the same section.

The intended final fix was:

- keep actor text tied to the direct legal unit;
- match a family inside the candidate authored unit, not the whole section;
- use authority and binding language for REPRESENTATIONS instead of the broad
  phrase `representations and warranties`; and
- keep Rule first, with only non-duplicate timing and qualification text.

The filesystem failed while these edits were in progress. Do not treat the
recovered lawyer packet as accepted legal output.
