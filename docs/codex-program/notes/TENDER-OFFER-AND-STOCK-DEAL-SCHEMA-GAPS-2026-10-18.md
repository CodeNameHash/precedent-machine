# Tender-offer and stock-deal provisions the V2 schema has no place for

Evidence for Ben, compiled 2026-10-18 by implementer C on the manager's work package C4 (plan finding (128)). Two agreements read in full from their EDGAR exhibits, no intake: GSK / Nuvalent (Agreement and Plan of Merger dated 2026-06-09, a two-step tender offer, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1861560/000119312526262950/d137459dex21.htm) and Indivior / Supernus (Agreement and Plan of Merger dated 2026-08-01, an all-stock merger of equals, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1356576/000110465926089425/tm2622009d1_ex2-1.htm). Checked against `contracts/product/legal-schema.v2.json` (the families, subtypes and required fact types), `docs/core/LEGAL-RULES.md` and `contracts/product/table-shapes.v3.json` (the rows and coded vocabularies), and against what the extractor did with the one tender offer the corpus has run (Gilead / Arcellx, V1 run 27dcf3e2 and V2 run f1e2493f on the preview branch).

Seventeen of the 34 candidate deals (plan finding (127)) are two-step tender offers and one is this stock merger; the corpus has met the form only through Arcellx's CVR tender offer.

## What the schema says today

- The word "offer" appears in the V2 schema twice, neither about a tender offer; "tender" never; "Exchange Ratio", "S-4" and "Parent stockholder" never. LEGAL-RULES.md has no line on the Offer. The extractor's prompt is built from the schema's family and subtype descriptions and the table shapes' guidance, so the extractor is never told what the Offer is.
- The table shapes know the form: `structure-mechanics-table` codes `dealStructure` as TENDER_OFFER_BACK_END_MERGER (decision 1) and `consideration-structure` codes ALL_STOCK (decision 18); `conditions-table` has an "S-4 / Proxy Effective" row and the votes table an AFTER_S4_EFFECTIVENESS anchor; `votes-approvals-meeting-table` has a "Parent / Merger Sub approvals" row; CLOSING_CONDITIONS has S4_EFFECTIVENESS and LISTING subtypes; the no-shop fiduciary vocabulary has FAIL_TO_RECOMMEND_AGAINST_A_TENDER_EXCHANGE_OFFER_WITHIN_THE_REQUIRED_PERIOD. The form is coded; the Offer's own terms are not.
- What the extractor did with Arcellx's Article I (the Offer), read from the stored rows: the V1 run routed 1.01 to CONSIDERATION and TERMINATION and wrote 26 facts, all PER_SHARE_CASH_CONSIDERATION; the V2 run (bundle V9) routed 1.01 to nine families and wrote 45 valid facts under CONSIDERATION_PACKAGE, CLOSING_CONDITIONS/GENERAL_CLOSING_CONDITION, PROXY_MEETING/DOCUMENT_FILING, GENERAL_COVENANTS/MERGER_SUB_OBLIGATION, FINANCING_COVENANTS/OBTAIN_FINANCING, TERMINATION/REMEDY_ENTITLEMENT and MERGER_STRUCTURE_CLOSING/TRANSACTION_STEP; Annex I (the Offer conditions) went to CLOSING_CONDITIONS/BRINGDOWN and GENERAL. The extractor files the Offer wherever a subtype half fits; the Minimum Condition, the Expiration Date and its extensions, the restrictions on amending the Offer and the Section 251(h) merger have no name to be filed under and no row to land on.
