How Corpus read it

Northstar / Aria. The Company pays 3.0% of its equity value within two business days after a termination under Section 8.1(b), and the fee is the sole remedy save for fraud.

Cobalt / Vela. The Company pays 3.5% of its equity value concurrently with termination, and specific performance survives alongside the fee.
