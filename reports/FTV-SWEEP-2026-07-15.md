# Force-the-Vote (FTV) corpus sweep — draft codebook input

Method (read-only, DB queried via `SUPABASE_URL`/`SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` from `.env.local`,
same as `scripts/reprocess.js`):

1. Pulled all 12,619 `provisions` rows (all 40 deals, all types) with `full_text` + `ai_metadata`.
2. **ai_metadata scan**: only 2 provisions in the whole corpus carry an explicit
   `features.forceTheVote` / `forceTheVoteDetails` key — both on QXO/TopBuild (see below and
   `topbuild-dedup-dryrun.md`). Everywhere else this feature was never extracted, so verdicts
   below are corpus-text-derived, not feature-derived.
3. **Text scan for FTV language**: layered regex passes across all provision types (not just
   NOSOL) for the "vote survives an Adverse-Recommendation-Change" construction — the two
   phrasings found in this corpus are (a) `Notwithstanding any Adverse Recommendation Change
   ... shall [nonetheless/still] submit/convene/call and hold ...` and (b) `a Change in the
   [X] Recommendation shall not limit [X]'s obligation to submit this Agreement to the
   stockholders ... for the purpose of obtaining the ... Stockholder Approval`. A plain
   "the Company shall submit this Agreement to its stockholders for approval" covenant with
   *no* ARC-override language (found in ~9 other deals, e.g. Marriott/Starwood, Quikrete/Summit,
   ConocoPhillips/Concho) was **not** counted as FTV — it's the ordinary vote-covenant
   boilerplate, not a force-the-vote override, absent the notwithstanding-ARC tie.
4. **Self-termination-for-Superior-Proposal check**: for every deal, checked `TERMR*`
   provisions category `Superior Proposal` (present for 31/40 deals) plus a corpus-wide fallback
   search (catches cases where the clause exists but was filed under `TERMR/Unclassified`,
   e.g. HPE/Juniper). Presence of a clause letting the target terminate *itself*, pre-vote, to
   enter an Alternative Acquisition Agreement (paying the fee) = self-termination right present
   → makes any FTV clause "soft." Absence = "hard" (board can flip its recommendation but the
   company can never escape the vote by walking away for a better deal).

Caveat: this is a keyword/regex sweep over stored `full_text`, not a clause-by-clause legal
read of all 40 agreements. Phrasing variance (e.g., "unless this Agreement has been validly
terminated" as the escape valve instead of "Notwithstanding any Adverse Recommendation Change")
means the "none" verdicts below are "no FTV language detected by this sweep," not a certified
absence — flag for spec-writer follow-up before this becomes a codebook rule.
